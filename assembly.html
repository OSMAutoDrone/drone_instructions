    Outils

      Préparer la structure

      Lay out the frame pieces. Check that the motor mounting holes are unobstructed. Use the to loosely fasten the arms.

      Mount the motors

      Position each motor on its arm. Secure it using the specified fasteners and the . Check that no screw reaches the motor windings.

      Check the completed assembly

      With the battery still disconnected and propellers still removed, check that every fastener is secure and that no wire can touch a moving part. Document the checks required before first power-up.
